# Understand

Understand before trying to build on top of what exists.

What existing implementations did you study?

What are their main architectural decisions?

Why were these decisions made?

What assumptions do they make?

What trade-offs exist?

What do you still not understand?

---

Additional questions:

- How does the system work?
- What's the architecture?
- What are the core mechanisms?
- What are the constraints?
- What cases does it fail on?
- If AI wrote or helped write part of this, can you explain every decision in it yourself? What part, if any, are you still unsure about?

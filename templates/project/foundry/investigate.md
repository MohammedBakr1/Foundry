# Investigate

Don't settle for "I understood it." Turn understanding into something testable.

```
Hypothesis → Implementation → Experiment → Measurement → Comparison → Conclusion
```

- What's the hypothesis you're testing?
- What experiment did you design?
- What measurements did you collect?
- How does your result compare to the original implementation or to your expectations?
- What's the conclusion?

Possible tools depending on the nature of the project: experiments, tests, benchmarks, profiling, comparisons, failure cases.

# Reflection

Decide: "What did I learn?"

Record the knowledge — the project becomes a Knowledge Artifact, not just source code.

**These questions are optional.** Answer whichever ones are actually useful to you, and skip the rest. There's no obligation to fill in every question for every project.

- What did I know before starting?
- What did I want to understand?
- What did I study?
- What projects did I refer back to?
- What decisions did I make?
- What failed?
- What did I discover?
- What's the difference between my implementation and the original?
- What did I learn?

---

## Next Step: Develop or Contribute

"What will I do with what I learned?"

**Develop** — if what you built has real value, keep developing it as an independent project (software, a tool, a library, a research project, a product, or even a commercial venture).

**Contribute** — or return the knowledge to the community (code, a patch, a feature, an optimization, a tool, documentation, a bug fix, a research result, an architectural idea).
